DEPARTMENT RANKS

Rank (0)

PD - Cadet

New academy recruit undergoing basic department training and legal orientation.

Rank (1)

PD - Police Officer

Full-fledged officer responsible for daily patrols, traffic control, and answering dispatches.

Rank (2)

PD - Senior Officer

Experienced patrol officer capable of supervising junior units and managing high-risk calls.

Rank (3)

PD - Sergeant

NCO supervisor directing scene operations, tactical formations, and unit assignments.

Rank (4)

PD - Captain

High command leader directing specialized divisions, recruitment, and internal administration.

Rank (5)

PD - Deputy Chief

Second-in-command overseeing entire department operations alongside the Chief.
